Project

Building-permit portals are valuable public records, but their labels, schemas, coverage windows, and geographic fields differ city by city. PermitKey harmonizes those records into a shared work-type vocabulary while retaining each source’s provenance and missingness.

The collection is intended as reusable infrastructure for studying construction, housing supply, energy upgrades, climate policy, and urban change. Counts are collected source records; repeated publications and multilabel work types are retained rather than silently deduplicated.

This site is the public project page. The data page will host reviewed aggregate tables, a data dictionary, taxonomy definitions, provenance, and validation notes when the release gates are complete.

Method

PermitKey follows a traceable pipeline: collect public records, preserve source-row provenance, normalize dates and locations, learn or crosswalk work types, and aggregate only after eligibility and disclosure checks.

Collect and preserve

Source registries, portal URLs, native categories, descriptions, dates, coordinates, and stable source-occurrence identifiers remain available in the private working copy.

Harmonize carefully

A documented 25-head candidate classifier adds work-type fields. Missing text is unavailable, not a negative; candidate scores are uncalibrated and the original structured-flag ensemble is not assumed.

Aggregate transparently

Planned products are permitting jurisdiction × year × work type and a companion 0.1° grid × year × work type for reliably geocoded records.

Data release

Preparation in progress

The copied collection and candidate pipeline have passed integrity smokes. Geography mapping, source-rights review, disclosure controls, and candidate validation remain before public downloads are enabled.

Open release status

The release will contain derived aggregates rather than raw permit-level records. Jurisdiction and grid products are distinct tables. Work-type counts can overlap because one source record may carry multiple labels.
